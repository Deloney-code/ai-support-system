Features

Features That Redefine Support

Everything you need to automate, organise and elevate customer support — powered end-to-end by AI.

AI Email Agent

An autonomous agent reads, understands and replies to customer emails 24/7 — in natural, on-brand language.

Smart Ticketing

Every conversation becomes a structured ticket — tagged, prioritised and tracked from first reply to resolution.

Real-time Dashboard

Live metrics on resolution rates, response times and agent load — so you always know how support is performing.

Auto-Resolve

Routine questions are closed instantly without human input — the AI drafts, verifies and sends the resolution.

Polish Reply

Turn rough notes into clear, empathetic responses. Agents draft fast; the AI refines tone, grammar and clarity.

Auto-Classify

Incoming requests are instantly categorised by intent and routed to the right team or AI flow — no manual triage.

How It Works

A fully automated pipeline that only involves your team when it truly needs to.
